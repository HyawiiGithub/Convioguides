02 / WHAT TO TALK ABOUT

Show the problem. Then show how Convio helps.

Use the real product as the source of truth. Make the value concrete, avoid vague AI hype, and never promise capabilities that the current product does not offer.

Angle 01 / Project Contract

Stop making every agent guess.

Explain how project decisions, architecture and permanent rules can be recorded once and reused as shared context.

Possible hook
“Your coding agent shouldn't have to rediscover your project rules every session.”
Angle 02 / Skills

Make expertise repeatable.

Show how skills can package procedures, questions, checks and validation into a process an agent can follow.

Possible hook
“A good workflow is more than a prompt. Give your agent a repeatable procedure.”
Angle 03 / One MCP connection

Less setup friction. A clearer workflow.

Introduce the idea of one MCP connection providing access to tools, skills, project context and pipelines.

Possible hook
“One connection for the capabilities your AI agent needs to get work shipped.”
Angle 04 / Approval gates

Automation with a human in control.

Highlight the review step before production changes, alongside pipeline validation and run history.

Possible hook
“Let the workflow run. Keep the final production decision with a person.”
03 / A SIMPLE WORKFLOW

Make consistent progress without posting for the sake of posting.

A practical loop for turning product updates and audience questions into clear marketing work.

STEP 01

Learn

Read the latest product updates. Test the feature or ask the team for clarification before describing it.

STEP 02

Choose one point

Pick one audience, one problem and one message. Avoid trying to explain every feature in a single post.

STEP 03

Publish or share

Prepare a useful post, short demo or community response. Follow each platform's rules and community norms.

STEP 04

Learn from results

Record reactions, questions, clicks and feedback. Use what you learn to improve the next piece of content.

04 / QUALITY BAR

Sound like someone who understands the product.

Specific, accurate communication builds trust. Overclaiming, spam and generic AI slogans weaken it.

DO Be specific and credible

  • Use the correct product terms: MCP connection, Project Contract, skills, pipelines and approval gates.
  • Show a real use case or workflow instead of listing features without context.
  • Check the current website and changelog before announcing an update.
  • Ask for clarification when a feature or claim is unclear.
  • Keep community outreach relevant, respectful and transparent.

AVOID Empty hype and invented claims

  • Don't describe Convio as a booking tool, CRM or marketing automation platform.
  • Don't promise integrations, pricing, automation or availability that hasn't been confirmed.
  • Don't use “revolutionary AI” language without explaining the actual benefit.
  • Don't spam unrelated servers, communities or people with repeated links.
  • Don't present a planned feature as already released.
05 / MEASURING PROGRESS

Look for signs of real interest.

Use these as signals to review, not as targets invented without team agreement.

Reach Relevant people saw the content, not just a large number of random views.
Engagement Replies, questions, saves and thoughtful discussion.
Intent Link clicks, demo interest, sign-ups or people exploring the product.
Learning Useful feedback and clearer understanding of what the audience needs.

Coordinate priorities, publishing access, approval requirements and any numerical goals with the team. This guide describes a sensible marketing workflow; the team's actual responsibilities and targets take precedence.
